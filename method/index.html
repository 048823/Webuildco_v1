The WeBuild Method

Scoped, built, proven, owned.

Stop renting automation. Own the system. Every engagement follows the same four steps — and ends with you holding everything.

01 · One session

Scope

A Foundations Session maps your workflows, finds the repetitive work worth automating, and writes the plan — scope, deliverables, payback math, fixed price. The plan is yours to keep, whoever builds it.

02 · Weeks, not months

Build

I architect and build the agents against your real systems — your CRM, your inbox, your calendar. Weekly updates. No surprises at handover because nothing is hidden before it.

03 · 2–4 weeks supervised

Prove

No agent gets the keys on day one. It runs supervised: it drafts, your team approves. As it proves itself, it earns autonomy — category by category, on your sign-off, never by default. The gates live in The Cage.

04 · Handover day + 30-day support

Own

You get everything: the code, the credentials, the runbook, the security config. Yours — to run yourself, or to have us keep managing. Own it, and choose who runs it.

What you get at handover

your-agent/
├── code & repo — versioned, documented
├── credentials — your accounts, your keys
├── runbook — how to operate, pause, and fix it
├── cage-config — the approval gates and rules
└── architecture notes — how and why it's built

If WeBuild vanished tomorrow, your systems keep working. That's the point.

About the method

Most single-workflow agents are live in 3–4 weeks: scoped in week one, built and tested against your real historical cases in weeks two and three, then run supervised until go-live. Bigger scopes take longer — and we say so in the written spec, not after the invoice.

Every install includes 30 days of post-handover tuning. After that you have two options: run it yourself with the runbook we hand over, or keep us on the optional retainer.
