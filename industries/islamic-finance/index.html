AI agents for Islamic finance providers

Shariah-compliant finance carries double obligations: everything conventional finance must do, plus a governance layer nobody else audits. Agents take the intake, document chasing, and reporting — your Shariah board keeps the rulings.

Where the hours go

KYC and application intake

Identity documents, income evidence, declarations — collected piece by piece, checked for completeness, chased when incomplete.

Compliance documentation

Every product decision needs its paper trail — for the regulator and for Shariah governance. Assembling it is repetition; certifying it is not.

Customer education

Murabaha, ijarah, takaful — customers ask the same foundational questions daily, and answering them well takes real time.

What the agents run

Workflow automation under your governance — the agent assembles and chases; your compliance and Shariah functions decide.

  • →Runs application intake — collects KYC documents, checks completeness against the product's checklist, chases gaps, and stages complete files for review
  • →Drafts compliance packs — assembles the document trail for each decision from your records, formatted for review — never self-certifying
  • →Answers product questions — from your approved knowledge base — your wording, your fatwa references, never improvised finance answers
  • →Keeps applications moving — status updates to customers, bottleneck alerts to your team
  • →Prepares reporting — recurring regulatory and board reporting drafted from your data, on schedule, for human sign-off
Compliance & data — read this part

Shariah governance and financial-services law both stay human

Two hard lines are built into every agent for this vertical. Regulatory: financial product advice is licensed activity — agents inform from your approved materials and never advise. Governance: nothing an agent produces carries Shariah authority — product answers come only from your board-approved knowledge base, wording is vetted (no interest-based framing, ever), and anything novel routes to your people. The agent is workflow infrastructure under your governance, not a voice within it. The full gate spec is published on The Cage page — bring whoever owns compliance to the call.

The split is simple: the agents take the repetitive work, your people keep the judgment. Nothing we build makes a decision a human should make — it drafts, checks, chases, and books, so your team does the work you actually hired them for.

Questions

Only from your approved knowledge base, in your approved wording — and it declines anything beyond it, routing the customer to your team. It never generates its own religious or financial rulings. That's a permanent gate, not a setting.

The knowledge base is built with your team so the agent speaks your terms correctly — and prohibited framings are enforced at the spec level, checked during the supervised proving run before anything customer-facing goes live.

We work in it — including a live engagement with an Islamic endowment institution. We won't claim scholarship we don't have: your Shariah board governs; we build the infrastructure that respects that governance.
