AI agents for Islamic charities and waqf institutions

Small teams, seasonal surges, and a trust obligation most organisations never carry. Agents take the donor admin and the coordination — your people keep the relationships and the amanah.

Where the hours go

Ramadan happens every year

Donations, questions, and campaigns spike for a month — but you can't staff for the peak, so the team burns out instead.

Donor questions repeat

Zakat eligibility, where funds go, receipt requests, project updates — the same answers, deserved by every donor, expensive at volume.

Institutional knowledge in heads

Processes, program history, board decisions — living in inboxes and memories, re-explained every time someone new asks.

What the agents run

Built for the way charities and waqf institutions actually work — small teams, seasonal surges, and a trust obligation most software ignores.

  • →Answers donor questions — from your organisation's own knowledge base — programs, processes, receipts — instantly and around the clock
  • →Handles surge intake — Ramadan and Dhul Hijjah volume absorbed without surge staffing, with anything sensitive routed to a human
  • →Chases and files paperwork — receipts, acknowledgements, program reports — drafted, tracked, and filed against the right records
  • →Keeps coordination moving — board follow-ups, program logistics, volunteer scheduling — the EA work nobody has budget for
  • →Drafts donor communications — updates and campaign follow-ups for human approval — your voice, your sign-off
Compliance & data — read this part

Donor trust is the asset — the build treats it that way

Charity money carries obligations: ACNC governance, donor privacy, and — for tax-deductibility claims — DGR status that must be verified, never assumed (agents are configured to make no deductibility claims your status doesn't support). Zakat carries its own handling rules, which stay with your people and your scholars. Donor data stays in your systems, and anything touching money is permanently human-gated. The full gate spec is published on The Cage page — bring whoever owns compliance to the call.

The split is simple: the agents take the repetitive work, your people keep the judgment. Nothing we build makes a decision a human should make — it drafts, checks, chases, and books, so your team does the work you actually hired them for.
