What we build

Your agents, in the apps everyone already uses.

No new portal to log into. No app for your clients to download. Your AI agents meet people on iMessage, WhatsApp, and Telegram — the threads they're already in all day.

The friction of "one more tool"

Every new channel you ask a client to adopt is a reason for them to go quiet. The fastest way to lose a warm lead is to make them create an account before they can talk to you. Internally, it's the same — your team won't check a system they have to remember to open.

What we build

Your agents, wired into the messaging your world runs on

Your agents, wired into the messaging your world already runs on.

Client-facing

Enquiries, bookings, and questions handled over WhatsApp, iMessage, or Telegram, in a natural back-and-forth, day or night.

Team-facing

Your people ask the agent for a status, trigger a job, or approve a draft from the same chat they already use.

One system, many channels

The agent is consistent whether the message came from a client on WhatsApp or a staffer on Telegram.

It does the work, not just the reply

It can research the lead, check the details, and draft the response before a human touches the thread.

Supervised until proven

Supervised and secure

Messages flow through The Cage with the guardrails you set. Sensitive actions wait for human approval until the agent has earned autonomy on that category. You decide what it can send on its own and what it drafts for review.

You own it forever

Owned by you

The integration, the logic, and the config are yours. Swap the model, change what's connected, extend it to another channel — no rebuild, no dependency on us.
