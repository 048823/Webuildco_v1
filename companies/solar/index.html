AI agents for solar companies

Solar leads are bought, shared, and gone in hours — while your best closer is on a roof. Agents take the speed-to-lead race and the rebate paperwork; your team takes the installs.

Where the hours go

Leads go cold in hours

Shared leads get called by every installer who bought them. Whoever makes contact first usually wins — and enquiries land at 8pm, not 8am.

Quote follow-up dies

The quote goes out, silence follows. Systematic day-2, day-7, day-14 follow-up wins real deals — and nobody has time to run it.

STC and rebate paperwork

Every install drags a trail: STC forms, state rebate applications, grid-connection docs, proof-of-completion — per job, per scheme, per state.

What the agents run

Minutes matter in this vertical more than almost any other — and after-hours is where the enquiries actually arrive.

  • →Answers every lead in minutes — qualifies (roof type, bill size, timeline), answers the first questions, and books the site assessment — 24/7
  • →Runs the follow-up cadence — every quote chased on schedule until it's a yes, a no, or a real objection your closer should hear
  • →Chases the install paperwork — rebate and connection documents tracked per job, customers chased for signatures, files staged for your submitter
  • →Keeps customers updated — install scheduling, what-happens-next, weather delays — proactively, before the frustrated call
  • →Feeds the CRM honestly — every touch logged, so your pipeline reflects reality instead of memory
Compliance & data — read this part

Rebate paperwork is compliance paperwork

STC claims and state rebate schemes carry real compliance weight — accredited-installer requirements, scheme-specific evidence, and lodgement rules that change. The agent's job is completeness and chasing: right documents, right job, staged for the accountable human who submits. Claims and certifications stay with your accredited people, and quotes always carry human-approved pricing before they leave the building. The full gate spec is published on The Cage page — bring whoever owns compliance to the call.

The split is simple: the agents take the repetitive work, your people keep the judgment. Nothing we build makes a decision a human should make — it drafts, checks, chases, and books, so your team does the work you actually hired them for.
