# Ongoing Operational Load Estimate

This model is front-loaded. The live service becomes low-touch only after call flow, test matrix, consent wording, escalation path, monitoring, and reporting are standardized.

## One-Time Setup Per Client

| Work | Hours |
| --- | ---: |
| Discovery: services, service area, booking rules, escalation policy | 2-3 |
| Prompt and tool configuration | 2-4 |
| Phone number setup, forwarding, summary delivery | 1-2 |
| Calendar/CRM integration if required | 3-6 |
| 30-call test pass and fixes | 4-8 |
| Privacy/recording disclosure and retention review | 1-2 |
| Client handover and supervised launch | 1-2 |

Lean demo setup: 8-12 hours.

Real first client setup: 15-27 hours depending on integrations and compliance review.

## Ongoing Monthly Load

| Call volume | Monitoring | Prompt/rule tuning | Client support/reporting | Total |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 50 calls/month | 0.5-0.8h | 0.3-0.5h | 0.5-1.0h | 1.3-2.3h |
| 200 calls/month | 1.0-1.5h | 0.5-1.0h | 0.7-1.2h | 2.2-3.7h |
| 500 calls/month | 2.0-3.0h | 0.8-1.5h | 1.0-2.0h | 3.8-6.5h |

After the niche and templates are standardized, target ongoing load should be:

- 50 calls/month: under 1.5h/client/month.
- 200 calls/month: under 2.5h/client/month.
- 500 calls/month: under 4h/client/month, with usage overage or a higher plan.

## Practical Monitoring Standard

For month 1:

- Review every failed or escalated call.
- Sample 20-30% of successful bookings.
- Check every booking/callback summary.
- Track missed calls, failed tool calls, caller frustration, and latency.

After stabilization:

- Review 10-15% of successful calls.
- Review 100% of emergency, angry, unsupported, and no-summary calls.
- Send a short monthly value report: calls answered, bookings, callbacks, escalations, failures, estimated recovered revenue, and next fixes.

## Margin Implication

At a A$500/month service fee, 500 calls/month leaves roughly A$323 before labour using the current infra model. That cannot support 5+ hours of monthly human attention. Either cap usage, price overage, or package higher-volume clients at a higher monthly fee.
